table.go, steps.go and suggest.go are copied from PalaTerm (github.com/kojio145/palaterm, MIT, same author)
internal/listimport at commit f3fb42d (2026-11-07), and the step and condition types of rule.go from its
internal/model/importrule.go.
Kept as a copy on purpose, so that the two tools can move at their own pace (spec 17.1).

Left out: plan.go (merging into PalaTerm's device list), guess.go (guessing its OS types), choices.go, and the
parts of the others about its device fields and passwords. New here: rule.go (a rule gives rows of the design
data, written as YAML), convert.go, draft.go and the step "cidr".
Fixes made here should be carried back by hand when they matter to PalaTerm too.

vars.go (the variable rules of spec 9.7.2) follows PalaTerm's 変数ルール as of v1.10.5 (internal/model/fillrule.go
and readFill and matchFillNames of app_fill.go): a row whose device cell is empty continues the device above, more
devices on a row, exclusions on a column or on a variable (a device left out takes its continuing rows with it),
and the match of device names (width, spaces, the domain part, "-" and "_"). Written here as YAML of a case folder.
